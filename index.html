The Philosophy

Crafted in Grasse,
aged in obsidian.

“We reject the vulgarity of loudness. True luxury whispers only to those permitted into your orbit.”

Founded in the 8ème Arrondissement of Paris with distillation quarters in Grasse, SILLAGE was conceived as an antidote to mass-market olfactory noise. Our compounding philosophy honors the ancient alchemy of maceration: allowing raw resins, wild oud, and rare floral concretes to mature in total darkness.

Alchemical Rigor

Extraction & Master Craft

01

Supercritical CO₂ Extraction

Dense liquid carbon dioxide under sub-ambient pressure isolates delicate floral fractions without destructive thermal stress or petrochemical solvents.

02

120-Day Charred Oak Maceration

Compounded elixirs mature for four lunar cycles in charred French Limousin oak casks, infusing the perfume with velvety wood vanillins and tannins.

03

Cold Filtration & Crystal Pour

Cooled to precisely -4°C to precipitate residual botanical waxes before decanting into lead-free crystal flacons with individually turned solid gold caps.
